Our story

Good questions
make better work.

We started Northwind in 2014 with a simple belief: the most useful design begins by paying attention. To people, to place, and to the small details that point toward something bigger.

Today, we’re a close-knit team of designers, thinkers and makers partnering with ambitious teams around the world. We stay intentionally small, bring the right minds to every brief, and make things built to last beyond a launch date.

Our home is Portland, Oregon. Our point of view is always in motion.
